# Proofline Product Strategy

## Thesis

Proofline helps customers establish what was checked, how it was checked, what evidence supports the result, and what remains unknown in an AI-assisted deliverable.

## Initial customer hypothesis

Small research and consulting teams that produce AI-assisted reports, spreadsheets, and dashboards. Validate the problem and willingness to pay before presenting it as a market fact.

## Differentiation hypothesis

Cross-artifact verification and a traceable evidence pack, not another chat UI or automatic truth certification.

## Initial wedge

Sell a fixed-scope verification service supported by a local-first toolkit. Start with CSV/data checks and evidence packets. Add workbook, research, and browser adapters only behind explicit acceptance criteria.

## Avoid for now

Multi-tenant hosting, broad agent orchestration, billing infrastructure, dozens of connectors, and complex automation before pilots show a repeated need.

## 30-day experiment

1. Consolidate and verify the source package.
2. Complete browser and export tests.
3. Interview likely customers.
4. Run scoped pilots using permissioned and redacted data.
5. Measure time saved, useful defects, false positives, and willingness to pay.
6. Decide which repeated manual steps deserve automation.

## Success gate

Consider SaaS only after real paid work shows repeat value that the local workflow cannot deliver efficiently.
