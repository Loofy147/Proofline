# Proofline Pilot Offer

One report, workbook, CSV or dashboard with agreed acceptance criteria; source/evidence review; deterministic data checks; independent recalculation where applicable; browser interaction/export tests where agreed; a reproducible handoff pack with PASS, FAIL, NOT TESTED, and BLOCKED states.

Excludes certification, exhaustive truth guarantees, hosted monitoring, and checks that could not run. Measure review time saved, useful defects, false positives, repeat demand, and willingness to pay.

Commercial status: hypothesis to validate with interviews and paid pilot jobs. No pricing or market-fit claim is established yet.
