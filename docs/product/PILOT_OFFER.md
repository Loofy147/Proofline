# Pilot Offer: AI Deliverable Assurance

## Who it is for

A small consulting or research team that uses AI to prepare a report, spreadsheet, CSV, or dashboard.

## Proposed fixed scope

One deliverable or closely related pack:
- agree the scope and testable acceptance criteria;
- review a defined set of factual claims and sources;
- run deterministic CSV/data checks;
- independently recalculate relevant workbook cells where possible;
- test agreed interface controls and exports;
- hand over findings, reproducible steps, failures, and limitations.

## Handoff

Evidence ledger, test results, source/input list, hashes where appropriate, reviewed deliverable, known failures, NOT TESTED items, and recommended fixes.

## Exclusions unless separately agreed

Certification, regulated advice, exhaustive truth guarantees, hosted monitoring, and checks that could not run or be reproduced.

## Success measures

Review time saved, useful defects detected, false positives, repeat use, customer trust, and willingness to pay. Demand and pricing remain hypotheses until validated.

## Data handling

Agree confidentiality, processing location, retention/deletion, sharing permissions, and redaction before intake. Prefer local processing and synthetic/redacted examples during early pilots.
