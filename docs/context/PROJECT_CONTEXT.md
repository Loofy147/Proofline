# Project Context

## Product thesis

Proofline is an evidence layer for AI-assisted deliverables. It should help answer: what was checked, how it was checked, which evidence supports the result, and what remains unknown?

## Initial customer hypothesis

Small research and consulting teams that already prepare reports, spreadsheets, data, or dashboards with AI assistance. This is a hypothesis requiring interviews and pilot work, not a proven market fact.

## First service wedge

One deliverable pack with agreed criteria; source/claim ledger; deterministic data checks; independent spreadsheet calculations where possible; browser interaction/export checks where agreed; transparent handoff report.

## Differentiation hypothesis

Cross-artifact verification and traceable evidence, not another general chat interface or a promise of automatic truth.

## Non-goals for now

Hosted multi-tenant SaaS, payment infrastructure, direct model APIs, general autonomous agents, email/calendar connectors, and scheduled execution.

## Decision rule

Prefer the smallest feature that can be tested with a customer. Revisit the roadmap after three paid pilot jobs or equivalent strong evidence of repeating demand.
